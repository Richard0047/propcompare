Applies To适用范围Challenge Package (Phase 2 & Funded); Instant Package (all phases)挑战套餐（第2阶段 & 资金阶段）；即时套餐（所有阶段）
Phase阶段Phase 2 onwards (does NOT apply to Challenge Phase 1)第2阶段起（不适用于挑战第1阶段）
The Risk Consistency Rule (RC Rule) requires that the risk per trade idea must remain consistent, capped at less than 2% of the initial account balance per trade idea (including floating loss, swap, commission, and fees).

Definition of a "trade idea":
• Trades on the same symbol in the same direction = one trade idea
• Multiple trades on the same asset/direction closed simultaneously = one trade idea
• Trades on different assets or different directions = separate trade ideas

Allowed: Multiple positions on different assets/directions with <2% risk each; sequential positions on same asset with <2% risk each

Not Allowed: Multiple positions on the same asset in the same direction with combined risk >2%

Special case: For 51010-NoPC Packages, the RC ratio is reduced to 1%. 风险一致性规则（RC 规则）要求每笔交易理念的风险必须保持一致，上限为初始账户余额的 2%（含浮动亏损、隔夜利息、佣金及手续费）。

"交易理念"的定义：
• 同一品种同一方向的交易 = 一个交易理念
• 同一品种同一方向同时平仓的多笔交易 = 一个交易理念
• 不同品种或不同方向的交易 = 独立的交易理念

允许：在不同品种/方向开多个仓位，每个风险 <2%；在同一品种上顺序开平仓，每次风险 <2%

不允许：在同一品种同一方向开多个仓位，合计风险 >2%

特殊情况：51010-NoPC 套餐的 RC 比率降低至 1%。
