# Prop Firm Consistency Rules Comparison

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the consistency rules for each prop firm, including the specific rule names, thresholds, applicable programs, and official source links.

---

## FTMO

**Rule Name(s):** Best Day Rule

**Applies To:** FTMO Challenge: 1-Step and FTMO Account: 1-Step

**Rule Details:**
> The Best Day Rule requires that your most profitable day (“Best Day”) does not exceed 50% of your Positive Days’ Profit on the account. To pass the FTMO Challenge: 1-Step or to be eligible for a Reward on an FTMO Account, your profit from the Best Day must not represent more than 50% of your Positive Days’ Profit. Exceeding the Best Day limit is not treated as a rule breach. However, you need to continue trading to generate additional profit until your Best Day (the most profitable day) represents 50% or less of your Positive Days’ Profit on the account.

## FundedNext

**Rule Name(s):** None (for Stellar Instant Account), General Trading Rules (for Stellar 2-Step, 1-Step, Lite Challenges)

**Applies To:** Stellar 2-Step Challenge (Evaluation Phase), Stellar 1-Step Challenge (Evaluation Phase), Stellar Lite Challenge (Evaluation Phase), Stellar Instant Account (No consistency rules apply)

**Rule Details:**
> Stellar 2-Step Challenge: Daily Loss Limit: 5%, Maximum Loss Limit: 10%, Minimum Trading Days: 5 Days, 5 Trades. Copy trading allowed between own accounts, prohibited between different individuals. Stellar 1-Step Challenge: Daily Loss Limit: 3%, Maximum Loss Limit: 6%, Minimum Trading Days: 2 Days, 2 Trades. EAs and third-party indicators are not allowed. Stellar Lite Challenge: Daily Loss Limit: 4%, Maximum Loss Limit: 8%, Minimum Trading Days: 5 Days, 5 Trades. Stellar Instant Account: No consistency rules. Traders have full flexibility regarding lot sizes, trade frequency, and daily performance.

## The5ers

**Rule Name(s):** Minimum Profitable Days, Inactivity Rule

**Applies To:** Hyper Growth, High Stakes, and Bootcamp Programs (Evaluation and Funded Phases for Inactivity Rule); High Stakes Program (Evaluation Phase for Minimum Profitable Days)

**Rule Details:**
> High Stakes Program: Traders must have a minimum of 3 profitable trading days, where a profitable day means closed positions generated at least 0.5% profit of the initial balance. All Programs (Hyper Growth, High Stakes, Bootcamp): Accounts without trading activity for more than 30 consecutive days will be expired/closed. News trading is allowed, with restrictions on bracket strategies for Hyper Growth and Bootcamp, and executing orders 2 minutes before/after high-impact news for High Stakes.

## E8 Markets

**Rule Name(s):** Best Day Rule, Minimum Profitable Days

**Applies To:** E8 One (Funded phase only), E8 One Crypto (Funded phase only), E8 Signature Forex (Funded phase only), E8 Signature Crypto (Funded phase only), E8 Pro (Funded phase only)

**Rule Details:**
> For E8 One & E8 One Crypto: 40% Best Day rule (no single trading day can exceed more than 40% of total generated profits). Net profit (payout share) must be greater than 50% of daily drawdown. For E8 Signature Forex & E8 Signature Crypto: 35% Best Day rule (no single trading day can exceed more than 35% of total generated profits). Minimum Profitable Days between each payout: 5 (a profitable day is considered as a day where the realized closed P&L is 0.3% or more). For E8 Pro accounts: 40% Best Day rule (no single day can exceed 40% of total profits). Minimum profit request must equal 4% of the initial or scaled account balance. Minimum of 4 profitable days, each with a realized closed PNL of 0.5% or more of the account balance.

## FXIFY

**Rule Name(s):** 20% Consistency Rule, 25% Consistency Rule, 30% Consistency Rule

**Applies To:** 20% Rule: Instant Funding Lite (Funded). 25% Rule: Crypto Instant Funded (Funded), Crypto 1-Phase (Funded), 2 Phase Static (Funded). 30% Rule: Lightning Plan (Challenge and Funded).

**Rule Details:**
> FXIFY has program-specific consistency rules. The 20% rule for Instant Funding Lite states the best day's profit can't exceed 20% of total profits. The 25% rule for Crypto Instant Funded, Crypto 1-Phase Funded, and 2 Phase Static programs limits the best day's profit to 25% of the total. The 30% rule for the Lightning Plan, applicable in both challenge and live stages, caps the best day's profit at 30% of the total. No specific consistency rules were found for the One Phase, Two Phase Classic/Standard, or Three Phase programs on the official website.

**Sources:**
- **Official Source:** [https://fxify.com/faqs/](https://fxify.com/faqs/)

## WeMasterTrade

**Rule Name(s):** Risk Consistency Rule

**Applies To:** Challenge Package (Phase 2 and Funded Phase), Instant Package (51010-NoPC Packages have 1% RC ratio)

**Rule Details:**
> The Risk Consistency Rule mandates that the risk per trade must remain consistent, capped at less than 2% of the initial account balance per trade idea. This includes floating loss, swap, commission rates, and fees. Trades on the same symbol and direction are considered a single trade idea. Multiple trades on the same asset in the same direction closed together are a single trade idea. The opening time doesn't matter, only simultaneous closing. Trades on different assets or different directions are separate trade ideas. Allowed: Opening multiple positions on different assets/directions with <2% risk each. Sequentially opening and closing positions on the same asset with <2% risk each. Not Allowed: Opening multiple positions on the same asset in the same direction with combined risk >2%. For 51010-NoPC Packages, the RC ratio will be 1%. Risk consistency does not apply to Challenge Phase 1.
